Double pendulum on two hinges

Two HINGE joints — each fixing a point and an axis, each leaving one freedom — assembled into the classic chaotic linkage. 400 frames of dt = 0.01 (t = 4), every one a real IDA step on the constrained DAE. The gold rings are the joints; their axes run along z, straight at you in this opening view, so drag to see them. Measured: the joints hold to |g| = 5.6e-8 over the whole recording. Energy wanders by 4.5e-3 against a scale of mgL = 14.7 — 3 parts in 10,000 — and the same mechanism run as ONE continuous 4 s integration instead of 400 separate steps holds it to 7.4e-5, because each step restarts the multistep solver at order one.
