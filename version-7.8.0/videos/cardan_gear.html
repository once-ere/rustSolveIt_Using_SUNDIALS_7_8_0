Cardan gears, and a straight line

A wheel of radius r inside a ring of radius 2r: every point on the wheel's rim travels along a straight line, a diameter of the ring. The hypocycloid of ratio 2 is degenerate -- it is not an approximation to a straight line, it IS one. The rolling is a GEAR joint: one row, holding the two bodies' turns about a shared axis in proportion, ratio 1 because the wheel turns once backwards for each forward turn of the carrier. It stacks on the bearing rather than replacing it, so the mechanism is HINGE 5 + HINGE 5 + GEAR 1 = 11 rows on 12 freedoms, leaving the one freedom a gear train has. The ratio must be rational: the honest constraint is on ACCUMULATED angle, which a quaternion cannot report, so the row is sin(q*theta_i + p*theta_j) with the angles wrapped -- which is faithful only because p and q are whole numbers, and an irrational ratio is refused rather than rounded. What the constraint buys, measured on this scene: impose the same relation as an initial condition instead and the rim leaves the line by 1.795e-3 against the gear's 1.059e-8; then lean on the wheel with a torque and the imposed version collapses to 7.468e-1 while the gear holds 1.152e-8. The outer ring is drawn for reference and nothing is attached to it -- the gear row, not any contact, does the rolling.
