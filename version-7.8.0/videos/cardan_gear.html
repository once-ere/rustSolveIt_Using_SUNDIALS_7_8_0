Cardan gears, and a straight line

A wheel of radius r inside a ring of radius 2r: every point on the wheel's rim travels along a straight line, a diameter of the ring. The hypocycloid of ratio 2 is degenerate -- it is not an approximation to a straight line, it IS one -- and Cardan gears were used to turn rotation into straight-line motion because of it. READ THIS BEFORE BELIEVING THE PICTURE: there is no rolling contact here. This joint set has CONSTRAIN, BALL, UNIVERSAL and HINGE, and not one of them couples a rotation to a translation, which is what rolling means. The outer ring is drawn for reference and nothing is attached to it. What holds the 2:1 relation is the START -- crank +1 rad/s, planet -1 rad/s, which for this radius ratio IS the rolling condition -- and with no gravity and no torque both rates are conserved, so it persists. The difference that makes is measurable, and is the point: a real gear pair would hold the line under any load, while here the ratio is only an initial condition, so error accumulates in it. Over 315 cold restarts, one per frame, the rim point leaves the line by up to 1.8e-3; the same 6.3 s run as one continuous integration holds it to 4.1e-5, a factor of 43. Watch the trail: a line, 2 units long, exactly 2r either side of centre.
