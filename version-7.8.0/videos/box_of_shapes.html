Mixed shapes in a rigid box

The regression scenario mixed_shapes_rattle_in_the_box_conserving_energy: a cylinder, a disk and a cuboid inside a rigid BOX 4 whose six wall slabs have inverse_mass = 0, with G = 0. 36 real CVODE rootfinding events in t = 3; measured |dE|/E = 3.4e-16, i.e. exact to the last bit. Gold arrows are the analytic contact normals, scaled by the applied impulse. |L| about the origin is NOT conserved here, and should not be: the immovable walls supply outside torque.
