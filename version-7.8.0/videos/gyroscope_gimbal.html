A gyroscope in a gimbal

Three HINGE joints on three mutually perpendicular axes: 15 rows on 18 freedoms, leaving exactly the three gimbal angles. Every body is concentric, so all three hinge axes pass through one point -- the rings only LOOK nested, and no centre moves further from the origin than 1.2e-34. Each RING lies in the plane containing the axis it swings on and the axis it carries, because a gimbal ring pivots about a DIAMETER; the rotor is the opposite case and is meant to be, its symmetry axis being what it spins about. Unlike the spinning top, gravity does nothing here: there the centre of mass hung a distance r from the pivot and weight had a lever arm, while here every centre of mass sits ON the pivot, so the arm is zero. A hinge transmits no torque about its own axis, which gives two exact invariants: nothing can torque the system about the vertical, so total L.y = 0.52688 is held to 1.4e-14; and the innermost hinge IS the rotor's spin axis, so its axial spin I3 w3 = 3.75 is held to 1 part in 100,000. Watch what the gyroscope does with the push: the whole assembly is started turning about the vertical at 1 rad/s, yet the outer ring never swings more than 7.43 degrees -- the rotation comes back out as a TILT, the rotor's axis swinging up to 16.33 degrees away at right angles to the push. The three joints hold to |g| = 5.0e-8.
