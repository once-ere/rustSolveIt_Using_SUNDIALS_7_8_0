Model: three HINGEs on three perpendicular axes — 15 rows on 18 freedoms, the three left being the gimbal angles. Two exact invariants: no hinge can transmit torque about its own axis, so L·ŷ (outermost, vertical) and the rotor's axial spin I₃ω₃ are both conserved. The whole assembly starts turning about the vertical at 1 rad/s; watch that rotation come back out as a TILT of the rotor axis, at right angles to the push.
